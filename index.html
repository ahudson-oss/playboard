    How it works

    1. Drag players into the starting set.
    2. Hit + Add step, then move players to where they go next.
    3. For curls, flares, and loops, switch to Curved and drag the player along the route you want (or hold Shift while dragging). Dragging in Straight mode turns a route back into a straight line.
    4. Drop the ball on a player to give it to them. Passes show as dashed lines.
    5. Press Play to watch it run. Full screen for film or the projector.
